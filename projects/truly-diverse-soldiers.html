Truly Diverse Soldiers

A mod for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord, written in C# with Harmony. Version 1.0.0, released on Nexus Mods.

What it changes

In the base game every recruit of a troop type shares the same build, and every face is reshuffled from battle to battle, so a big army looks like a crowd of copies. The mod gives each soldier an appearance that stays the same every battle. A share of regular soldiers, 30 percent by default, spawn as women who are built that way from the start, with the right body, animations and voice. They take their faces, hair and tattoos from the women of their own culture. Weight and build also vary more within each troop type, and each culture keeps its character, so Sturgians stay broad and Khuzaits stay lean, just less uniform.

Why I made it

After only a few hours of playing I could tell the base game wasn't great but the mod community was strong. The armies did not behave realistically. Troop decision making felt like a couple of rule sets stacked together, and basic things like using spears in a shield wall and then switching to swords did not work. There was quality of life stuff too, like every troop being male and faces changing every time they spawned. This mod is my answer to those last two.

How it works

Each soldier is generated from a seed built from the party, the troop type and the soldier's position in the party, so the same position always produces the same soldier. Because of that, the mod saves nothing in the game save, which makes it safe to add or remove in the middle of a campaign. The cost is that the game only tracks how many of each troop a party has, not the individual soldiers. When soldiers die or get upgraded, the later ones slide into new positions and their looks change.

Playing alongside other mods

Many players run long mod lists, so the mod never changes the player character, companions, lords or any other hero, and it leaves alone troops whose gender or face another mod has already set. I tested it with a list of more than 100 mods, including Dynamic Troop Equipment, Culture Diversity Mod and Living Armies. It works in field battles, sieges and hideouts.

Three settings live in one configuration file: the share of women, how much body build varies, and whether faces stay exactly identical every battle or come from the game's lighter face cache, which only looks similar. If the file is missing, the mod falls back to its defaults.

Limits and what comes next

Custom Battle has no persistent parties, so looks are random there. Height is not varied yet, and that is the next thing I would add. After that I would look for ways to reduce how often looks shift when soldiers die or get upgraded.
