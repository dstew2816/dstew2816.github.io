Truly Diverse Soldiers

A Bannerlord mod that gives every soldier a lasting identity, so armies stop looking like clones.

Status
Released, v1.0.0
Game
Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.4.8
Built with
C#, Harmony

The problem

In the base game, every recruit of a troop type shares the same build, and every face is reshuffled from battle to battle. Soldiers have no lasting identity, so a large army reads as a crowd of clones.

Who it is for

Bannerlord players, many of whom run large mod lists. That shaped the requirements: it has to coexist with other mods, be safe to add or remove mid-campaign, and be easy to configure.

Three decisions that shaped it

I derive each soldier from a stable seed instead of saving anything.

Each soldier's gender, face and build come from a seed hashed from the party, the troop type and the soldier's slot, so the same slot always produces the same soldier. That means the mod stores nothing in the save, which makes it safe to add or remove mid-campaign. The cost is that the game only tracks how many of each troop a party has, not individuals. When soldiers die or are upgraded, later soldiers shift into new slots and their appearance changes. I state that limit on the mod page instead of hiding it.

I build soldiers correctly from the start.

A configurable share of regular soldiers, 30% by default, spawn as women built that way from the start, with the right body, animations and voice, rather than being swapped after they appear. Their faces, hair and tattoos come from their own culture's women, and every culture keeps its character: Sturgians stay broad and Khuzaits stay lean, just less uniform.

I leave heroes and other mods' choices alone, and let players pick the tradeoff.

The player character, companions, lords and every other hero are never changed, and troops whose gender or face another mod has already set are left as they are. Many mods depend on heroes, so touching them would break the load orders this audience runs. For performance, one setting lets players choose between identical faces every battle and the game's lighter face cache, which only looks similar. Three settings in one config file cover everything, and the mod falls back to defaults if the file is missing.

Results

Released as v1.0.0 on Nexus Mods. It works in field battles, sieges and hideouts, and I tested it alongside a load order of more than 100 mods, including Dynamic Troop Equipment, Culture Diversity Mod, Living Armies and RBM.

  • v1.0.0 first release
  • 100+ mods in the test load order

Known limitations, stated on the mod page: soldier appearance shifts when slots change, Custom Battle is random each time because it has no persistent parties, and height is not varied yet.

What I would do next

  • Vary height as well as weight and build, since it is the main thing still uniform.
  • Reduce how often appearances shift when soldiers die or are upgraded.
  • Read the player feedback after release and prioritize fixes by what people actually report.
