Automation Program Management InternPhoenix, Arizona, May to August 2026

I interned at TSMC's Arizona fab on the team that maintains the automation platform and procedures. I proposed an audit of the runs that never started, scoped it, and led it.

More on what I did

The procedures came from inconsistent templates, so I turned them into detection specifications and found owners for them across several departments and with teams in Taiwan, where I worked in Mandarin. I wrote SQL and Python against more than 1,000,000 daily log entries and set up on-call alerts for critical misses.

I sized the gap at about 600 hours a month of manual work and a conservative estimate of upper six figures a month in scrap and rework avoided, with wafers worth about $25,000 each, and prioritized the fixes that protect wafer flow. I also built a reusable audit template.

I shipped a platform-wide key performance indicator, time saved per run, along with four Power BI dashboards that are now in active use. The indicator was approved after I presented it to the head of manufacturing for Fab 21.
